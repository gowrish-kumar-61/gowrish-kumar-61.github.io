About me

I'm Gowrish Kumar Reddy, a prefinal-year B.Tech CSE (AI/ML) student at SRM Ramapuram. I work on vision models, mechanistic interpretability, and AI alignment & safety — and I'd like to keep working on them for a long time.

Most of my time goes into small, self-contained experiments: build the thing from scratch, poke at it until something surprising falls out, write down what happened. I'd rather deeply understand one attention head than skim ten papers.

how I got here
“You cannot control something you don't understand.”
— from an anime film

Honestly, the hook was almost accidental. I was watching anime, and that line stuck with me.

When I looked at where AI actually breaks down, it's exactly that: we're building systems more capable than our ability to explain their internals. Interpretability is the only domain that confronts that gap head-on.

So for me it's less “I read a paper and got convinced” and more that one line reframed how I saw the whole field — and I chased it from there.

what I'm interested in
Mechanistic interpretability

Finding the circuits inside a model and proving they do what you think they do.

AI alignment & safety

The gap between capability and explanation, and what closing it would take.

Vision models

Small VLMs especially — what they can do on hardware people actually own.
